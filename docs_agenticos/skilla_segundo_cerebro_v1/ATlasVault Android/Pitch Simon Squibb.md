
## 1. What's your dream?

> My dream is to build **Skilla**, a freelance marketplace that gives talented people in Angola and across Africa the opportunity to earn a living through their skills.
>
> Today, many freelancers struggle because there is no trusted platform for finding clients, secure payments, or building a professional reputation. Most work happens through WhatsApp or personal connections, making it difficult to grow.
>
> I want to change that by creating a platform similar to Upwork, but designed for African realities, making freelancing safer, more accessible, and creating opportunities for thousands of young professionals.
>
> I believe talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. Skilla exists to bridge that gap.

---

## 2. What help do you need beyond the money?

> Beyond funding, I need mentorship from experienced startup founders, introductions to investors and technology partners, guidance on scaling a marketplace business, and access to AI development tools.
>
> I also need technical resources like a reliable laptop, stable internet access, and cloud infrastructure to build and launch the platform efficiently.
>
> Learning from people who have successfully built global startups would be just as valuable as financial support.

---

## 3. What would the funding unlock and what would you spend it on?

> The funding would allow me to build and launch the first production version of Skilla.
>
> It would cover:
>
> • A development laptop
> • Reliable high-speed internet
> • AI development tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot)
> • Hosting, domain, databases and cloud services
> • UI/UX design improvements
> • Legal registration and operational costs
> • Initial marketing to attract freelancers and businesses
>
> This support would let me focus full-time on building the product instead of struggling with limited equipment and connectivity.
>
> My goal is to launch an MVP, validate the business model, and begin helping freelancers earn sustainable income.

---

## Se houver um último campo como "Why should we fund you?"

Podes responder:

> I'm a young developer and designer from Angola building a solution for a problem I've experienced personally. I know how difficult it is for talented people to access opportunities.
>
> I'm not asking for someone to build my dream—I already started. I'm asking for the tools and guidance to build it faster and create opportunities for thousands of others.
>
> I believe Skilla can become the platform that empowers Africa's next generation of freelancers.

### Uma pequena melhoria que pode destacar o teu pitch

Em vez de dizer apenas que queres criar um "Upwork africano", mostra o impacto:

> **"My dream isn't just to build another freelance platform. My dream is to give thousands of talented Africans access to opportunities they would never have otherwise."**

Essa frase comunica propósito, algo que o Simon Squibb costuma valorizar bastante.

O teu pitch fica mais forte se demonstrar que não estás apenas a pedir recursos (computador, Wi-Fi e IA), mas que esses recursos são os meios para construir uma empresa que pode gerar oportunidades para muitas pessoas.
